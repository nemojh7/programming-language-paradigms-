Or maybe it could happen if an incoming connection arrives, and then is closed again before you call accept. (Pretty sure in this case Linux still lets the accept succeed, but this kind of edge case is exactly where I'd be suspicious of a weird platform doing something weird.) You'd want to check these things.
Non-blocking mode is much more reliable because in the worst case, accept just reports that there's nothing to accept.
